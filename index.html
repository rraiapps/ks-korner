The Three Branches of Government 🏛️

The Who, What, Where, When and Why of how the United States is run.

🌟 The Big Picture

In 1787, leaders met in Philadelphia and wrote the U.S. Constitution — the plan for our government. They split the government into three branches so that no one person or group gets too much power. This is called separation of powers.
📜 Legislative = MAKES laws🦅 Executive = CARRIES OUT laws⚖️ Judicial = INTERPRETS laws

Tap a W to compare all three branches! 👇

🤝 Checks and Balances

Each branch can check (limit) the others — like teammates keeping each other fair:
  • The President can veto (say no to) a bill from Congress.
  • Congress can override a veto with a 2/3 vote.
  • The Supreme Court can decide a law doesn't follow the Constitution.
  • The President picks Supreme Court justices, but the Senate must approve them.
